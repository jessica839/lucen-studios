Data We Collect

When you visit this website, the following data may be automatically collected by our hosting provider (Vercel):

IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referrer URL, date and time of access, and pages visited. This data is processed for the purpose of operating the website securely and analysing usage. It is not used to identify individual users. Server logs are kept for a short period only. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating the site securely (Art. 31 nDSG).

Fonts, Currency & Images

Fonts. All typefaces are self-hosted on our own server. No font files are loaded from Google Fonts or any other third-party font CDN, so no data about your visit is shared with a font provider.

Currency & region detection. To present prices and content in a sensible currency, a script may call ipapi.co, a geolocation service that receives your IP address to estimate your country. The IP address is used only for this lookup; we do not store it for this purpose. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in presenting the site appropriately (Art. 31 nDSG). See ipapi.co's privacy policy.

Images. Some images are served by the Unsplash image CDN (images.unsplash.com); loading them transmits your IP address to Unsplash as a technical necessity of displaying the image. See Unsplash's privacy policy.

Contact & Booking

If you book a call or make contact through our scheduling tool (powered by GoHighLevel / LeadConnector), personal data you provide — such as your name, email address, and any details you submit — will be processed for the sole purpose of scheduling and conducting that call. We will not share this information with third parties, and we will not use it for unsolicited marketing without your explicit consent.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we process the email address you provide in order to send you practical AI and automation insights. The newsletter is handled through our CRM and scheduling platform (GoHighLevel / LeadConnector). We send it only on the basis of the explicit consent you give when subscribing, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter or by emailing us. We do not use your address for any other purpose. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6 nDSG).

Google Analytics 4

This website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Ltd (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). GA4 helps us understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

Consent first. GA4 runs under Google Consent Mode v2 and is set to denied by default. No analytics cookies are set and no data is sent to Google until you actively accept analytics in the cookie banner. You can withdraw consent at any time using "Manage cookie preferences" below.

Cookies and data. Once you accept, GA4 sets the cookies _ga (expires after 2 years) and _ga_* (holds session state). IP addresses are processed in truncated form and are not stored by GA4; Google Signals and advertising features are disabled, so your data is not used for cross-site advertising. Analytics data is retained for up to 14 months, then automatically deleted.

Legal basis & transfers. Processing is based on your consent (Art. 6 nDSG). Google LLC is certified under the Swiss–US and EU–US Data Privacy Frameworks, which provides an adequate safeguard for transfers to the United States. A data processing agreement with Google is in place.

For more on how Google handles data, see policies.google.com/privacy.

Under Swiss data protection law (nDSG), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to rectification: You may request that inaccurate data be corrected.

Right to erasure: You may request that your data be deleted, subject to legal retention obligations.

Right to object: You may object to the processing of your data for certain purposes.

Right to data portability: You may request the personal data you have provided to us in a common, machine-readable format (Art. 28 nDSG).

We want gamperklimmek.com to be usable by as many people as possible. As a small private business we are not subject to a statutory accessibility-statement requirement under Swiss law, but we aim — voluntarily — to meet the spirit of the WCAG 2.2 AA guidelines.

What we do

Semantic HTML and a logical heading structure; keyboard navigation with visible focus; sufficient colour contrast; descriptive alternative text for meaningful images; respect for the operating-system "reduce motion" setting; and a responsive layout that works across screen sizes and zoom levels.

Some embedded third-party tools — such as the GoHighLevel booking widget — are rendered by their providers, and their accessibility is outside our direct control. If one of them blocks you, email us and we will help you complete the same action another way.
